Welcome to VisorVR

You are looking at a panel. Everything VisorVR shows in your headset is one of these — delete this panel once you have your own.

1

Add a panel

Add panel in the sidebar. A panel can be a web page (a SimHub dashboard, a timing screen, a chart), a window from your desktop, or a file or folder of documents.

2

Place it in VR

Select the panel, then Edit in VR. Put your headset on and grab the panel with the mouse to move, tilt and resize it. It stays where you leave it.

Can't see it? Press Recenter, next to Edit in VR — a panel keeps its last position, so it may be behind or above you.

3

Bind a button

In the headset the keyboard is out of reach. Under Settings → Input, bind three buttons on your wheel, HOTAS or controller: Show/hide to clear the overlay away, Recenter VR to pull panels back in front of you, and Next panel to cycle between them.

Flying DCS World? Add the mission, briefing, aircraft kneeboard and radio log panels from Settings → Panels.
